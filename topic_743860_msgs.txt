3529980              2026-09-29 09:28:40.543000      2  <p>Hi everyone,</p>
<p>I have been receiving DM's asking how I managed to make my logistic regression have a high CV. For me, I have this basic "recipe" that I follow (not always applicable):</p>
<ol>
<li>If linear model, then provide nonlinear features as much as possible</li>
<li>If nonlinear model, then provide linear features</li>
</ol>
<p>For example, we can create a feature that mimics a tree node split  (if X &gt; threshold, then Y value etc). The reason for this is linear models do not inherently discover arbitrary nonlinear relationships by itself, so feature engineering matters alot more. You can even try more complex features as well such as conditionals, e.g.,   (X &gt; threshold) AND (category == A).</p>

3529122              2026-09-27 12:04:27.233000     16  <p>Hi everyone!</p>
<p>As this competition is almost over, I would just like to share my current hill climb AUC vs. public LB AUC correlation.</p>
<p><img src="https://www.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/kaggle-forum-message-attachments/o/inbox%2F31568110%2F53c33e3cdc20596c3dd3a561b9b44134%2Fhc_vs_public_lb.png?generation=1790509839023642&amp;alt=media" alt=""></p>
<p>My current best single model is XGB with a 10-fold CV AUC of 0.94671 (although, I am not really sure if I can call it single model considering I used predictions [target &amp; non-target] from inner-fold basic models as features). What I noticed quickly (which led to me having ensemble AUC of around 0.94680+) is that for models that led to a higher AUC improvement when ensembled with the main optimized model, they usually have low correlation with that main optimized model.</p>
<p>As such, I changed my approach accordingly in two streams:</p>
<ol>
<li>Try my best to squeeze out more AUC from the main XGB model</li>
<li>Create more diverse models (<a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/playground-series-s6e9/discussion/740769" target="_blank">taking inspiration from Tilii's discussions, such as here</a>).</li>
</ol>
<p>By diverse models, I just use this quick criteria for me to accept it in my ensemble pool:</p>
<ol>
<li>Minimum CV AUC of 0.9400+</li>
<li>Spearman correlation of less than 0.99</li>
</ol>
<p>All in all, for the diverse model pool, the main thing I am optimizing is not the CV AUC but rather, I am minimizing the correlation between those models and the main. It doesn't really guarantee (for me) that lower correlation = better ensemble, as in the case of one model with CV AUC 0.94416 and spearman correlation coefficient of 0.967 not being chosen by my HC scripts for the stack.</p>
<p>Thanks and good luck!</p>
